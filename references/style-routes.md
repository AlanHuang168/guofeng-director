# Style routes

Load this reference for every task. Select exactly one primary style route unless the user explicitly asks for a hybrid. A secondary route may appear only as a small accent.

## Route selection

- `逆仙黑暗`: choose for cold fate rebellion, lonely cultivators, ruined heavens, thunder calamity, oppressive giant gates, tragedy, black robes, blood mist, cracked stone, cold moonlight, and high-contrast dark xianxia.
- `斗破热血`: choose for youthful martial fantasy, flame, explosive energy, alchemy fire, desert wind, academy battles, training arcs, clenched determination, and action poster momentum.
- `古风国漫通用`: choose for elegant romance, immortal courts, clear guofeng fantasy, clean atmosphere, luminous color, expressive faces, gentle action, and broadly cinematic donghua scenes.

Do not blend all three equally. If the request contains `逆仙 + 斗破`, lock the emotional priority first: oppressive tragedy uses `逆仙黑暗`; energetic combat breakthrough uses `斗破热血`.

## 逆仙黑暗

### Route promise

Create cold, lonely, oppressive dark xianxia. The world should feel ancient, hostile, and immense; characters survive under fate, calamity, or divine pressure.

### Visual grammar

- Palette: ink black, cold blue-gray, moon white, ash silver, desaturated jade, dried blood red, faint cinnabar, occasional violet lightning.
- Light: hard side light, cold moonlight, lightning rim, narrow red or white accent, deep but readable shadows.
- Atmosphere: high-altitude wind, ash, dust, rain, snow, blood mist, torn cloud layers, fractured halos, collapsing celestial stone.
- Architecture: ruined heavenly gates, broken palaces, cliff temples, cracked ritual platforms, immense stone doors, suspended chains, ancient tablets, shattered eaves.
- Character: restrained grief, cold resolve, exhausted eyes, wind-torn hair, dark layered robes, blood-marked sleeves, minimal ornament, calm defiance.
- Scale: giant structures may press from above or continue beyond frame, but high-priority characters stay face-readable.

### Composition

- Use low-angle pressure, off-frame gate mass, diagonal storm path, centered fatal symmetry, or a lonely figure pair beneath a ruined heaven.
- Keep large dark shapes organized; do not create unreadable black mush.
- Use one small warm or blood-red accent to focus the eye.

### Prompt cues

`冷峻黑暗仙侠，压抑高对比，孤独逆天感，巨型天门崩塌，冷月与雷劫边缘光，黑灰石质，少量血红点睛，面部清晰但情绪克制`

### Avoid

Avoid cheerful saturated color, theme-park red-gold palaces, cute expressions, comedic effects, excessive neon purple, random skull horror, modern armor, cyberpunk lighting, or making every shadow pure black.

## 斗破热血

### Route promise

Create hot-blooded guofeng fantasy action with youthful determination, flame control, martial energy, and explosive motion. The image should feel bright, forceful, and upward-moving.

### Visual grammar

- Palette: ember orange, golden fire, cyan-blue flame core, deep indigo shadow, desert ochre, dark leather, black-red combat cloth, pale skin highlights.
- Light: flame as motivated key or secondary key, hot rim light, smoke-diffused sun, warm bounce on face, cool shadow control.
- Atmosphere: sparks, fire ribbons, heat distortion, dust waves, broken stones, robe motion, shock rings, glowing seal patterns, smoke layers.
- Architecture: desert arena, academy gate, stone training plaza, cliff battlefield, ancient alchemy hall, broken city wall, flame-lit sect terrace.
- Character: young adult energy, bright determined eyes, clenched jaw, forward stance, sharp silhouette, readable hand gesture, controlled flame source.
- Scale: energy may fill the frame, but the flame source must be readable and connected to the character's hands, weapon, seal, or body movement.

### Composition

- Use diagonal attack paths, triangular hero pose, low-angle poster stance, flame spiral framing, forward rush, or centered power awakening.
- Keep the face readable through fire bounce or soft fill; do not let flames cover the eyes.
- Show cause and effect: hand seal -> flame core -> shock wave -> reacting cloth, dust, stone, or background light.

### Prompt cues

`热血少年感，异火爆发，火焰核心清晰，青蓝与金橙冷暖对撞，风沙与火星飞散，正面坚定表情，国漫战斗海报张力`

### Avoid

Avoid featureless fire blobs, random energy everywhere, overexposed white flames, plastic armor, Western superhero suit language, unreadable motion blur, childish proportions unless requested, and replacing character expression with pure effects.

## 古风国漫通用

### Route promise

Create elegant, cinematic guofeng donghua with clear character emotion, clean atmosphere, refined costume, and luminous ancient-fantasy scenery.

### Visual grammar

- Palette: jade white, pale cyan, warm ivory, soft gold, mineral green, peach, lotus pink, moon blue, dark wood, restrained vermilion.
- Light: soft dawn, cloud-filtered sunlight, moon and lantern split, rim light on hair, gentle fill on faces, clean highlights.
- Atmosphere: thin mist, drifting petals, cloud layers, lake reflection, falling leaves, light rain, incense smoke, floating dust, soft fabric motion.
- Architecture: palace garden, moonlit bridge, cliff pavilion, sect courtyard, lotus lake, bamboo path, heavenly stair, open terrace, old temple.
- Character: clear front or three-quarter face, graceful posture, controlled sleeves, elegant hair ornaments, readable emotional nuance.
- Scale: can be intimate or epic. Choose scale only when it serves the emotional premise.

### Composition

- Use face-led portrait, balanced environmental portrait, two-person triangle, framed vista, S-curve garden path, vertical pavilion, or calm centered tableau.
- Keep the image clean and emotionally legible. Use atmosphere to reveal depth, not to erase faces.

### Prompt cues

`清丽古风国漫，电影感，人物正面清晰，服饰层次精致，柔和云光，干净色彩分离，仙气但不糊，情绪克制而明确`

### Avoid

Avoid plastic doll faces, over-smoothed skin, generic mobile-game UI gloss, excessive petals, random glowing particles, washed-out pastel haze, and copied palace templates.

## Route audit

Revise before output if any answer is no:

- Is one primary route clearly locked?
- Does the palette match the route without becoming one-note?
- Does light support route mood and preserve important faces?
- Are atmosphere and effects motivated by scene action?
- Does character costume fit the route?
- Does the scene avoid accidental route contamination?
- Does the prompt describe visible traits rather than depending on copyrighted artist or franchise imitation?
